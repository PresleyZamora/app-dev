The story is about Rakuro Hizutome, a high schooler obsessed with beating "trash games". One day, he decides to try a well-polished, top-tier VR game: Shangri-La Frontier, a massive multiplayer fantasy game with 30 million players. 

Challenges after another, he crosses paths with powerful NPC's, unlocking many side quests that many players dream of beating and achieving. He wanders through the game world with the help of his friends trying to unravel the 7 colossi Unique Monsters to conquer.
